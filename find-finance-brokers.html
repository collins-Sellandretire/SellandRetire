Why Use a Finance Broker

Financing a business sale isn't like a home loan

Acquiring or financing a business sale involves cash flow assessment, goodwill valuation, vendor finance terms and lender appetite that a standard home loan broker won't be across. A good finance broker knows how to structure a deal that lenders will actually approve.

The finance brokers listed here specialise in business acquisition and retirement sale transactions — they understand the deal mechanics, not just a standard lending checklist.

Looking for a business broker instead? See the difference →
🎯

Lender panel access

Finance brokers maintain relationships across multiple lenders and can match a deal to the right lender faster than going direct to one bank.

💼

Deal structuring

A good finance broker helps structure the right mix of debt, vendor finance and equity so the deal actually gets approved.

🤝

Lender negotiation

Having an experienced finance broker negotiate terms with lenders on your behalf can materially improve the rate and structure you end up with.

📋

End-to-end process management

From finance application to approval to settlement — a finance broker manages the process so you can focus on the business.
